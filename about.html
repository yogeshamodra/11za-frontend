Who we are

Making Business Communication Simple.

11za is a technology platform built to help businesses manage and grow customer conversations on WhatsApp.

We started with a simple observation: as businesses grow, customer conversations grow with them. Managing those conversations across personal phones, spreadsheets and disconnected tools can quickly become difficult.

11za brings everything together in one place. Our focus is not just on technology. It's on solving real business problems with tools that are simple to use, easy to scale and built around the way businesses communicate today.

  • Organize every customer conversation in one place
  • Empower your team to reply together
  • Automate repetitive tasks and follow-ups
